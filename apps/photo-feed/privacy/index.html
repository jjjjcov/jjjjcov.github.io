memscroll

Privacy Policy

memscroll is designed to let you rediscover photos and videos already in your Apple Photos library.

Effective September 13, 2026

Photos library access

memscroll requests permission to access the photos and videos you choose to make available through Apple Photos. That access is used to build and display the feed and to support actions you request, such as marking an item as a favorite or opening the iOS share sheet.

Your photos and videos are not uploaded to or stored on servers operated by memscroll. If an item is stored in iCloud rather than on your device, Apple Photos may download it using Apple's services.

When capture details are visible for an item that contains location metadata, memscroll may send its coordinates to Apple's MapKit geocoding service to obtain a city-level place name. memscroll does not display or persist precise coordinates or street addresses. The resulting city label is held temporarily in memory. Apple's handling of information sent to its services is governed by Apple's privacy policies.

Information stored on your device

memscroll stores app preferences on your device, including feed filters and order, autoplay and mute choices, and the last viewed item. This information is not sent to memscroll.

Changes you make to your Photos library, such as favoriting an item, are saved by Apple Photos and may sync through iCloud according to your Apple settings.

Sharing

memscroll only presents Apple's share sheet when you choose to share an item. The destination you select is responsible for its own privacy practices.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

memscroll does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software. memscroll does not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can change or revoke memscroll's Photos access in iOS Settings. You can remove preferences stored by memscroll by deleting the app. Deleting the app does not undo changes already saved to your Photos library.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as memscroll changes. The effective date above will be revised when an updated policy is published.
